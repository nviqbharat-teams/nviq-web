## Step 6: Google Business Profile (most calls)

### Rules (avoid suspension)
- Create a profile **only for a real address where you or a partner actually serve customers** (fitment centre, partner RTO consultancy office). No virtual offices, no fake addresses.
- Business name = your real name on signage (e.g. "NViQ VLTD Fitment Centre"). Do not stuff keywords into the name.
- Primary category: **GPS supplier**. Secondary: *Vehicle inspection service*, *Car security system installer*, *Auto electrical service*.
- Phone: +91 92570 38711 (or the partner's own number for partner locations).
- Website: the city link from the table below (it carries `utm_source=gbp`, so leads show up as GBP in GA4 and the backend `attribution.channel`).

### Profile checklist (each location)
- [ ] Services: "AIS 140 VLTD fitment", "Panic button installation", "VLTD certificate on Vahan", "School bus GPS", "Taxi GPS", "Mining VLTD (DMG)" (where applicable), "Fleet GPS tracking"
- [ ] 10+ real photos: shop front, technician fitting a device, panic button, certificate screen (blur customer details)
- [ ] Hours, service area (the RTO districts you cover)
- [ ] Do not add prices. Add a "Call for today's price" line in the description
- [ ] Q&A: add the top 3 FAQs from the website (is VLTD mandatory, how fast is the certificate, do you come to the yard)
- [ ] 1 post per week: a recent install (with permission), a rule update, a fleet offer

### Reviews (biggest ranking factor)
Ask **every** customer right after the certificate is issued. WhatsApp message to copy:

> **Hindi:** नमस्ते {name} जी, NViQ से आपकी गाड़ी {vehicle} का VLTD सर्टिफिकेट Vahan पर अपडेट हो गया है। अगर हमारा काम अच्छा लगा तो 30 सेकंड में Google पर रिव्यू दें: {review_link} 🙏
>
> **English:** Hi {name}, the VLTD certificate for {vehicle} is now updated on Vahan. If you were happy with NViQ, please leave us a 30-second Google review: {review_link}. Thank you!

- Get `{review_link}` from GBP → "Ask for reviews".
- Target: 10 new reviews/week per location. Reply to every review within 24h (thank them, mention the city + service).
- Never buy reviews, never offer discounts for reviews. Google removes them and can suspend the profile.

## Step 8: Backlinks

### A. Partner badge (fastest, most relevant links)
Every RTO agent, dealer, school and transporter that works with you can add the badge from **https://www.naviqbharat.com/partner-badge**.
It links to their exact RTO page with anchor text such as "AIS 140 VLTD fitment in RJ-14 (Jaipur)".

WhatsApp message to partners:

> **Hindi:** नमस्ते {name} जी, NViQ पार्टनर होने के नाते आप अपनी वेबसाइट पर "Authorised NViQ VLTD Partner" बैज लगा सकते हैं, जिससे ग्राहकों का भरोसा बढ़ता है। 1 मिनट का काम: https://www.naviqbharat.com/partner-badge → अपना RTO चुनें → कोड कॉपी करके वेबसाइट के फुटर में लगाएं। मदद चाहिए तो इसी नंबर पर बताएं।
>
> **English:** Hi {name}, as an NViQ partner you can add the "Authorised NViQ VLTD Partner" badge to your website. Customers trust it, and it takes a minute: https://www.naviqbharat.com/partner-badge → pick your RTO → copy the code into your site footer. Reply here if you need help.

Track it: every badge link carries `utm_source=partner_badge`.

### B. Listings (one-time, all free)
- [ ] IndiaMART, Justdial, TradeIndia, Sulekha: business listing, link to `/vltd` or the city page
- [ ] State transport / bus-operator / truck-union associations (RJ, UP, HR, PB, DL, GJ, BR): member or vendor listing
- [ ] School associations / school transport contractor groups: vendor listing (link `/gps-tracking/school-bus`)
- [ ] Dealer networks (commercial vehicle dealers you supply): "VLTD partner" on their site (link `/gps-tracking/vehicle-dealer`)

### C. Content people link to
- [ ] Monthly "AIS 140 rules update" post (new state notifications, deadlines). Send it to local news / transport blogs / YouTube creators in the trucking niche
- [ ] Share the free tools (`/tools/ais-140-checker`, `/tools/fitness-checklist`) in transporter and school WhatsApp groups. The checklist has a built-in "Share on WhatsApp" button

### Don'ts
- No paid link packages, PBNs or link exchanges. They get penalised.
- Don't use the same exact anchor text everywhere. The badge varies it per RTO automatically.
